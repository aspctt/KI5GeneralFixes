KI5 General Fixes patches problems KI5's vehicles share, rather than one car at a time.

They are built from a common template, so a fault in the template is a fault in every vehicle built on it. Fixing them one by one means a hundred near identical patches to keep in step. This targets the shared surface instead: fix it once, and every KI5 vehicle in your load order gets it.

Nothing here touches a vehicle that is not KI5's, and every fix stands down quietly when the thing it patches is not installed.

[b]Build 42.20+. Singleplayer and multiplayer. Safe on an existing save.[/b]


[img]https://i.ibb.co/xtVSd8Kn/Steam-Workshop-Contents-Seperator.png[/img]

[b]What it fixes:[/b]

[list]
[*]The stutter as a vehicle streams into range, and the [i]cannot get ID for container[/i] spam behind it
[/list]

[b]Vehicle container ids.[/b] The engine knows nine vehicle container names, hardcoded, and they are all vanilla's own. Every trunk, roofrack and seat a modded vehicle adds fails that lookup, and the game writes a line to two log files each time it does, once per container on every loot roll. That is what stutters when a car loads in.

It also means no loot table can be written for those containers at all, because the lookup a distribution matches on never resolves. This registers them properly, 371 of them across every vehicle mod installed while building it, and nothing spawns or changes hands as a result. It is an engine gap rather than a KI5 one, so it covers any vehicle mod that hits it.

Each further fix is listed here as it lands, with what was wrong and which vehicles it reaches.

Where another mod already does one of these jobs, this one gets out of the way.
