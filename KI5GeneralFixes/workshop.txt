description=[b]Vehicle container ids.[/b] The engine only knows nine vehicle container names, hardcoded, and they are all vanilla's own. Every trunk, roofrack and seat a modded vehicle adds fails that lookup, and the game writes to two log files each time, once per container on every loot roll. That is the stutter when a car loads in.
description=
description=It also means no loot table can target those containers at all. This registers 371 of them. Nothing spawns or changes as a result.
